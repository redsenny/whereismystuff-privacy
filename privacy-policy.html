Privacy Policy

Where Is My Stuff?  ·  Last updated: May 25, 2026

Where Is My Stuff? ("the app") is a personal item-tracking app. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data Stored on Your Device

All item information you save — including photos, descriptions, and saved locations — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

Location

The app requests access to your device's location to:

  • Record where you saved an item (when you tap "Use Current Location")
  • Show your distance from a saved item
  • Point the compass arrow toward a saved item
  • Alert you when you are near a saved item (Pro feature)

Location data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted externally. The app does not track your location in the background (except for nearby alerts, which you can disable in iOS Settings).

Camera & Photos

The app requests camera and photo library access so you can attach photos to saved items. Photos are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded or shared.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Where Is My Stuff? offers an optional Pro subscription managed through Apple's App Store. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. The app also uses RevenueCat to verify subscription status. RevenueCat may associate an anonymous identifier with your purchase to confirm entitlements. No personally identifiable information is shared with RevenueCat.

Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications for parking reminders and nearby item alerts (Pro features). You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools are used
  • No advertising identifiers are collected
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
